Liz Palmer Bastian

Cofounder

Liz Palmer Bastian, a devoted mom and bonus mom to eight incredible kids whose life has been shaped by grief, resilience, love, and service.

Liz was widowed at 31, with four young children. Their ages were 7, 5, 4, and 2. She quickly realized that her children needed additional love and support after the devastating loss of her husband and their father, Cameron. She looked for male role models to surround her children with and it wasn’t enough. She saw that her children needed community. They needed other children that were in similar situations as theirs, so Liz started hosting hikes, park days, and other family activities in search of building this connection for her children. This was the start of The Chosen Connection that now serves over 60 families a year, and building lifelong memories for hundreds of children.

As a single mother, Liz earned her Bachelor of Science in Family Science from Utah Valley University, where her passion for strengthening families and building meaningful connections took root.

Liz remarried and joined families with her husband, Marcus. Marcus is a widower with 4 children of his own and has increased her household size to 10! The whole family is actively involved in The Chosen Connection as her children still attend activities and prepare to be mentors in the summer program.

Whether she’s advocating for grieving families, raising a beautifully blended family, or sharing wisdom learned through life’s unexpected paths, Liz brings authenticity, compassion, and a unique perspective to the organization.

Georgia Allred

Social Media Specialist — Board Member

Georgia Allred is a wellness coach, speaker, and wholehearted believer that healing and laughter can coexist. Her relationship with grief started early—at 11, after losing her two-year-old brother—and deepened when she became a widow at 32, raising three young children on her own.

Life didn’t exactly ease up after that, but somewhere along the way, Georgia learned that even in the hardest seasons, light still finds a way in (sometimes with a little help from dark chocolate) 😝

Since 2008, she has worked on and off as a coach, helping others navigate grief, heartbreak, and life’s unexpected plot twists. Her approach is grounded in compassion, intuition, and real-life experience—not just theory. She holds a degree in Marriage and Family Studies and continues to invest in her own growth, because she knows you can’t guide others somewhere you’re not willing to go yourself.

Rooted in her faith as a member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, Georgia finds joy in family, friendship, singing, dancing, hiking, and anything outdoors. Her mission is simple: help others heal deeply, live fully, and find the joy in every day.

Dale VanWagoner

Volunteer Coordinator — Board Member

Dale is a southern boy having grown up in Alabama and North Carolina before moving to Utah while in High School. He served a mission to Mexico and after returning home married his eternal companion Coby. They built a family with 3 kids and a home filled with love, life and baseball.

In 2012 Coby was diagnosed with Breast Cancer and after an 8 year battle, passed away September 2020.

Since the loss of his wife of 25 years, Dale has found enjoyment in spending time with his now 3 granddaughters and family, hiking, playing pickleball and most surprising, learning to dance!

While the future has not been what was planned, there is so much to be grateful for in living a life that is filled with adventure, learning, friends, family and above all the gospel. The knowledge of knowing that this is not the end and we all will be reunited with our loved ones that we have lost can be such a comforting and peaceful understanding.

He has loved seeing his kids grow, see his family grow and see how he has grown. While he would still love nothing more than to have Coby there to share in all that is his current life, he recognizes dwelling on it is not productive and looks forward with hope and excitement to what is just around the corner.

One of the favorite things that he has found is serving those that have lost a parent. Having lost his own father at only 13, he understands the confusion and need to have connection with others who have been through the same thing. This is why he works with The Chosen Connection, assisting parents in providing opportunities for their kids to find that they are not alone in their grief.

Brynn Clinger

Activities Coordinator — Board Member

Brynn Clinger is a dedicated family advocate with a lifelong passion for strengthening families and helping youth discover their purpose and joy. Holding a degree in Family and Human Development, Brynn has spent the last nine years working within the public school system, serving as a trusted resource for children and parents alike.

Her heart for nonprofit work is deeply enriched by her personal journey of resilience. After navigating the profound loss of her husband, Matthew, to extra-skeletal osteosarcoma in 2021, Brynn turned her personal grief into a mission of deep empathy. Today, she walks alongside others in their own grief journeys, providing a safe, compassionate, and judgement-free space for individuals to feel, process, and heal.

Through every high and low, Brynn remains defined by an unshakeable love for life, a passion for staying active, and a firm belief that laughter is often the best medicine. When she isn’t advocating for others, she is busy managing her five “Wild Clingers”—proudly wearing her title of “crazy mom.” An outdoor enthusiast at heart, she loves keeping on the move by camping, paddleboarding, sports, and sharing time and big laughs with the people she loves most.

Our Mission

Building connection through shared experience

The Chosen Connection was created to ensure no child feels alone in their journey through grief. We believe that when kids who share similar experiences come together, something powerful happens — they realize they're not the only ones, and they find the courage to keep going.

Through outdoor adventures, community events, and volunteer-driven programming, we give families a place to belong and children a reason to smile.
